# Beginner experience report

## Biggest strengths

1. **No wall at home** — Dashboard loads immediately; onboarding is a banner, not a trap.  
2. **Explicit “first win”** — Quick quiz path under ~2 minutes is real.  
3. **Start Here** explains *how* to use the app in plain steps.  
4. **Daily minimum** surfaces concrete “done for today” signals.  
5. **AI fallbacks** — Beginner isn’t stuck when API is offline.  
6. **Failure recovery** — Quiz/PBQ/Boss don’t dead-end into shame loops.

## Biggest risks

1. **Lesson page density** — Video + highlights + brain book + training blocks = high cognitive load before confidence builds.  
2. **Security jargon** — Even “simple explanation” assumes literacy the user may not have yet.  
3. **Readiness %** — Looks like a grade; beginners may think “I’m 40% a person.”  
4. **Two imports** — Progress backup vs author lesson JSON still confuses some users despite copy.  
5. **Search** — Requires knowing *what* to type; zero-knowledge users may not have tokens.

## Where a beginner might quit

- First open of **Lesson** on mobile (scroll exhaustion).  
- First **low practice exam** score without reading recovery copy.  
- **Cleared storage** without ever exporting.  
- **AI** appears “broken” (no key) and they think the whole app is.

## Top 10 beginner improvements (impact order)

1. **One-sentence hero** on Dashboard: “Practice Security+ in Messer order—quizzes, videos, and a coach on what to do next.”  
2. **Collapsible “advanced”** on Lesson (labs/sims behind “I’m ready for hands-on”).  
3. **Hover/long-press glossary** for first occurrence of acronyms in lesson titles.  
4. **Readiness tooltip** — “Local practice score—not a pass guarantee.”  
5. **Post-quiz micro-celebration** even on wrong answers (“You logged a gap—fixing gaps is the job”).  
6. **Guided first day** checklist (3 items max) pinned until complete.  
7. **Video offline** friendly text when embed fails.  
8. **Search** empty state suggests domain numbers (“try `phishing`, `ACL`, `802.1X`”).  
9. **Export nudge** after first quiz pass.  
10. **Beginner mode** copy pass — fewer simultaneous concepts on screen.

## Score (beginner readiness)

**74/100** — Safe and kind in failure paths; main gap is **early cognitive load** and **numeric readiness misread**.
