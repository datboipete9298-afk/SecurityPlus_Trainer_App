Scoring reflects a **polished local-first trainer** with strong curriculum tooling, validators, SOC/PBQ depth, optional AI with structured fallbacks, and serious trust positioning. ## What blocks 100 (explicit)

1. **No formal exam environment** — timing, Pearson UI, adaptive exam mechanics are approximated at best here.
2. **Human discipline** — no app guarantees study minutes; retention scores cap without calendar integration users opt into.
3. **Vendor variance** — mobile browsers differ for PDF picker, IndexedDB quotas, clipboard.
4. **AI stochasticity** — even perfectly prompted models can waffle; structural JSON + timeouts mitigate but cannot eliminate variance.

---

## How to revisit scores later

Raise **Overall** sustainably by: narrowing primary pathways, extending offline tutor substance, reinforcing PDF checkpoint discipline, PBQ/mobile QA on real devices, and keeping validators green as data grows—not by inflating rhetoric.
