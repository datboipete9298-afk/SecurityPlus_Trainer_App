# Exam prep quality report (Security+ coach lens)

## Does this help someone pass?

**Yes, as a structured rehearsal layer** — if the student also uses official objectives, one primary video course, and realistic expectations. The app **does not replace** CompTIA or hands-on PBQ interfaces.

## What is strong

- **Messer-ordered path** with locks → reduces “random YouTube” sprawl.  
- **717-question bank** with explanations and trap language.  
- **Multi-select** where data models support it — trains careful reading.  
- **Exam mode** on practice exams (no per-Q AI, deferred explain) builds timing discipline.  
- **Weak area + domain score** nudges create a repair loop.  
- **PBQ-style labs** train *procedure memory* (high exam ROI skill).  
- **Boss fights** = spaced, harder retrieval with a clear pass threshold.  
- **Review-only wrong sets** after exams.

## What is missing (vs “complete exam system”)

- **Official PBQ UI fidelity** — by design; still a gap vs test-day ergonomics.  
- **Timed full-length exam clock UX** — partial (user can use wall clock; app doesn’t simulate 90-min container).  
- **Performance-based drag-drop** variety beyond ordering (if exam uses other interaction types).  
- **Print / offline packet** mode for airline study.  
- **Explicit objective → question mapping UI** for obsessive auditors.

## What could mislead

- **Readiness %** if read as “probability of pass.”  
- **High quiz %** on repeated items → familiarity ≠ transfer.  
- **AI explanations** when live — must be verified against lesson/explanation text.

## What should be clearer (copy-level)

- Near readiness: **“Heuristic from this device’s practice—not a guarantee.”**  
- On PBQ hub: **“Trains thinking; not a copy of proprietary screens.”** (partially present — reinforce near attempts.)

## Before exam day — test checklist

1. One full **Exam A–C** in true exam mode without notes.  
2. **Review every miss** until explanation feels boring.  
3. **PBQ labs** until ordering is fast without guessing.  
4. **Weak domains** &lt;50 → drill until &gt;55 for confidence.  
5. **Sleep** — the app cannot optimize this; say it anyway.

## Score (exam prep quality)

**86/100** — Strong rehearsal + repair; official materials and test-center discipline still required.
