# Product + retention audit

## Value proposition

**Clear for insiders** (“Messer order Security+ trainer with coach + exams”).  
**Opaque for cold traffic** — friend opening link needs one plain sentence above the fold.

## Would a friend understand it?

**After 60 seconds:** mostly yes if they read the dashboard purpose line.  
**With zero instructions:** medium risk — they may not know “Messer order” matters.

## Would someone come back tomorrow?

**Yes if:** streak hooks them, daily minimum incomplete, or spaced cards due.  
**No if:** they felt scolded by readiness, lost draft exam, or cleared storage.

## Daily minimum

Helps **closure** (“done today”) — good retention mechanic **if** the card stays visually prominent.

## Identity lines

Can **help** serious students feel progression; can **feel fake** to skeptics — tone is already restrained vs gamified apps; still watch frequency.

## Streak fairness

Streak logic is **local and transparent enough** if user reads Progress; hidden algorithms would hurt trust — current design is OK.

## Backup nudge + resume

**Strong retention pair** — reduces “I’m afraid to close the tab” anxiety.

## What would increase retention?

1. **Email-free weekly summary** (export reminder) — future.  
2. **“Next best 10 min”** single CTA mode.  
3. **Social accountability** optional share card — future.

## Recommendation score

**Would recommend to a serious peer:** yes, with disclaimers about official materials.  
**Would recommend to total novice:** with supervision.

**Retention / stickiness: 79/100**
