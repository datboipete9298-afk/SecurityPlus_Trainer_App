# Learning Science Master Audit

| Principle | Present? | Where | Weak? | Strengthen |
|-----------|----------|-------|-------|------------|
| **Active recall** | ✅ | Quizzes, PBQs, flashcards, teach-back micro | Some users skip teach-back | Micro-prompt after 2 wrongs same concept |
| **Spaced repetition** | Partial | `spaced` queue + nextReview | Not FSRS-level | Acceptable for MVP; document “light SRS” |
| **Interleaving** | Partial | Practice exams, mixed readiness accent in `nextStepEngine` | Linear lesson default | Mix mode toggle on practice page? (future) |
| **Retrieval practice** | ✅ | Quizzes flashcards | Over-highlighting risk | Coach “close PDF” nudge |
| **Feedback loops** | ✅ | Explanations, missed journal, weak routing | Emotional tone | Positive microcopy on repair |
| **Metacognition** | Partial | Confidence selector on some flows | Coverage uneven | Extend to quiz systematically |
| **Cognitive load** | Mixed | Lesson page dense | Simple lesson mode helps | ✅ |
| **Mistake-based learning** | ✅ | Missed journal → re-quiz, cards from misses | Busy users dodge | ✅ Smart coach |
| **Transfer** | Partial | Sims, SOC elite labs good | Lecture-only path weak | Mandatory hands-on cue in strip |
| **Habit / streak** | ✅ | streak, daily minimum | Punitive framing? | Celebrate small wins ✅ cards |

Honest takeaway: strongest on **immediate feedback loops** + **scenario transfer** via training block; weakest on rigorous **SRS** and calibrated **confidence / interleaving** compared to dedicated spaced-repetition apps.
